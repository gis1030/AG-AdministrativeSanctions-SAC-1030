# Statistical study of administrative sanctions recorded in 2024 in the municipality of Schaerbeek

This statistical study analyzes the recording and application of Municipal Administrative Sanctions (SAC) in the municipality of Schaerbeek during the year 2024. Designed as a key tool to relieve congestion in the criminal justice system and provide an immediate response to behaviors that harm civic coexistence, the SACs allow for a quantitative evaluation of the dynamics of civility, cleanliness, and mobility within the territory managed jointly with the Brussels North Police Zone.Link

## Details

During this period, a total of 13,734 infractions corresponding to 9,304 unique offenders were recorded, reflecting a constant sanctioning activity throughout the year without significant seasonal fluctuations. On the financial side, these sanctions generated a billed volume of €1,060,961, of which an amount of €839,051 has already been "Collected", demonstrating high efficiency in the management of municipal recovery with a collection rate of 79.1%. Through the analytical decryption of this data, this report examines the distribution of the most recurring infractions in order to identify geospatial and temporal trends that will serve as a basis for designing preventive and structural public policies in the municipality.Last Update
